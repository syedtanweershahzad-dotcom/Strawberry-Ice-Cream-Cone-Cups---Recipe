Strawberry Ice Cream Cone Cups

(0) SKILL LEVEL: Beginner

A dessert dream come true, a golden blondie cup topped with a scoop of velvety ice cream, a drizzle of sweet TANWEER'S Strawberry Syrup, then topped with a sweet surprise - a TANWEER'S KISSES Strawberry Ice Cream Cone Candy. It's the perfect recipe that encapsulates all of those delicious summer flavors in every bite.

Ingredients & Directions

Yields: 12 blondie cups
Prep: 20 min.
Bake: 18 min.
Total: 38 min.

Bake Mode:

Prevent your screen from going dark while you bake.

Ingredients

Strawberry Ice Cream Cup:

  • Vanilla or strawberry ice cream
  • TANWEER'S Strawberry Syrup
  • Pink and white sprinkles (optional)
  • TANWEER'S KISSES Strawberry Ice Cream Cone Candy

Chewy Blondie Cups:

  • 1 ⅓ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ⅛ tsp salt
  • 6 Tbsps butter or margarine, melted
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 tsps vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs

Directions

1. Bake Chewy Blondie Cups: Heat oven to 350°F. Line 12 muffin cups (2-1/2 inches in diameter) with paper or foil baking cups.
2. Stir together flour, baking powder and salt; set aside.
3. Stir together melted butter, brown sugar and vanilla in large bowl.
4. Add eggs; stir until well blended.
5. Stir in flour mixture, blending well.
6. Fill each prepared muffin cup about 1/2 full with batter.
7. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until tops are lightly browned and wooden pick inserted in centers comes out clean.
8. Remove from oven; with back of spoon immediately press indentation into top of blondie to form shallow cup.
9. Cool completely in pan on wire rack.
10. Assemble Strawberry Ice Cream Cups: Remove paper liner from blondie cup; place on dessert plate. Top with ice cream, strawberry syrup and sprinkles, if desired. Garnish with TANWEER'S KISSES Strawberry Ice Cream Cone Candy. Serve immediately.
